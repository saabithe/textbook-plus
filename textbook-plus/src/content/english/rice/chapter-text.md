About the Author
Wangari Maathai is a Kenyan environmental and political activist. In the
1970s, Maathai founded the Green Belt Movement, an environmental
non-governmental organization which focussed on the planting of trees,
environmental conservation and women's rights. In 1986, she was awarded
the Right Livelihood Award, and in 2004 she became the first African
woman to receive the Nobel Prize for her contribution to sustainable
development, democracy and peace. ‘When a Sapling is Planted’ is her Nobel Prize
Acceptance Speech at Oslo, December 10, 2004.
